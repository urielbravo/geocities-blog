Everyone who built a site in 1997 had a hand-written page and a scanned jpeg for
a background. That was the whole toolchain. The list on the resources page is the
modern equivalent — free, no account, and immediately useful.

The one I use most is happyhues, because picking a palette that does not fight
itself is genuinely hard to do by eye.
