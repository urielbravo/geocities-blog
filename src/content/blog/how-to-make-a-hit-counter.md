---
title: "How To Make A Hit Counter"
date: 1995-09-03
category: "technology"
imageLabel: "A seven-segment counter display"
excerpt: "Every page needed one. Mine was a transparent GIF that could not be fooled, which is a longer story than it sounds."
---

Every page needed one, and everyone had one that read 000042 forever because
nobody had worked out how to write the number back.

The working version was one transparent GIF per digit, swapped server-side on
each hit. It counted reliably and it looked exactly like 1995 was supposed to
look.

> A counter that does not move is a decoration, not a counter.
