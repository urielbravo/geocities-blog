The whole aesthetic is a constraint exercise. Everything on this site is either a
real 1997 asset or a CSS approximation of one, and the theme rules came first —
the layout had to survive the theme, not the other way around.

The hard part is restraint. Beveled borders and hard shadows look like a period
detail in isolation, and like a mess in quantity. The buttons in the sidebar
work because there are only three of them.

> Pick one period and commit. Half measures read as neither.

- Hard 3px borders, no blur, no gradients
- Courier for structure, Georgia for reading
- One neon colour, used sparingly
