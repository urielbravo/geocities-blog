Everybody's first instinct was to buy the faster modem. Almost nobody's first
instinct was to stop doing the things that were making the line slow.

- Turn off the phone's own dial tone, it eats a second of handshake
- Close the other modem line before dialling
- Download one thing at a time, seriously
- Check the phone cable for a kinked third pair

> The fastest modem in the house was the one that was not sharing a line.
